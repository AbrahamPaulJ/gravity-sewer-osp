Sim 3: growth and sensor placement

Where would new homes show up in the sewer's water level, and where would a few sensors see most of that growth, whatever the unknowns turn out to be?

A point-in-time picture: the growth is added all at once to today's network, with no pipe upgrades. In reality the dwellings arrive over three to five years, which is why the time-to-notice figures matter as much as the millimetres.
