# Ninh's topology-aware placement against Sim 4's simulation-based placement

Generated by `tools/ninh_compare.js`. Uncalibrated, steady-flow model: these figures compare methods on the same runs; they do not forecast.

## Method

- **Ninh (2025), greedy.** A sensor at manhole s sees a blockage at a downstream manhole v if Z_v + 0.7·H_v > Z_s + H_s (Eq. 1, ε = 0), with H_s the dry-day water depth. A covered manhole covers the pipe leaving it; coverage is pipe length (37.55 km, 890 pipes, 328 candidate manholes). The paper uses a genetic algorithm; this uses greedy maximum coverage. No simulation is used to place the sensors.
- **Ninh (2025), genetic algorithm**: the paper's own search on the same objective.
- **Coverage greedy (2-pipe radius)**: the general coverage model. Each sensor covers every manhole within 2 pipes of it, up or down the network; greedy adds the manhole that covers the most new ones. Topology only.
- **Sim 3.5, region-less**: simulated growth (Sim 3's runs), sensors chosen from every manhole with no regions; the exact set that most warns, a build-out step ahead, of the first new surcharge in the hardest wet case (`simulation_src/sim35_warn.py`).
- **Sim 4, Greedy: average** (current) and **Greedy: worst case**: chosen on simulated growth, within the regions to monitor or watch.
- **Scored on Sim 4's 504 runs.** Growth monitoring: a sensor rises 10 mm or more. Overcapacity, surcharge, overflow: the first build-out step at which a manhole newly crosses 0.7 of pipe height, the pipe crown, or the lid, per weather case and region. *At the time*: a sensor rises 10 mm in that run. *Early*: a sensor had risen 10 mm one step before. *At the spot*: a sensor sits at a manhole that crossed.
- **Two scopes.** Scenario coverage is Sim 4's test: growth in its target regions (monitor or watch), up to the 2041 homes. Growth monitoring and the events count every region and every build-out step. Sim 3.5 chose from every manhole with no regions, so it does better on the second and worse on the first.
- Scenario coverage figures for Greedy: average match Sim 4's published ones (5 sensors: 88% worst, 94% average).

On this steep network Ninh's rule cannot reach full coverage: a sensor in every one of the 328 manholes covers **75% of the pipe length**, because a backup 0.7 of a manhole deep reaches only a short way up a steep pipe (the paper notes the same for steep systems). Reaching that maximum takes 179 sensors; half of it, 37; 80% of it, 92.
