3. Hydraulic Equations & Viscosity Formulation

Full-bore Manning Conveyance: Q_full = (1 / n_eff) · A · R^(2/3) · S^(1/2)

Viscosity Roughness Correction: n_eff = n_0 · [1 + 0.12 · ln(ν / ν_0)] where ν_0 = 1.05 × 10^-6 m²/s.

Mean Travel Velocity: v = Q / A_flow (m/s), Travel Transit Time: τ = Σ (L_i / v_i).

Pump Station Mass Balance: dV_well / dt = Q_inflow - Q_pump, rated 50 L/s at PS-01 with VSD 0–150%.

Blockage Capacity Reduction: Q_choked = Q_cap · (1 - Blockage%)^1.8.
