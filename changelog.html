25 October 2026, later still

Simulation 4: filling corridors, the new default placement new

Level sensors are now placed on the filling corridors: chains of manholes whose outlet pipe goes past 80% full as growth is added, in any wet run, not only the runs that surcharge. These are the paths along which the network runs out of room. Among them the sensors are still chosen to warn before the first manhole surcharges. The previous method stays on the page as "Before surcharge, any manhole".

25 October 2026, later

Simulation 4: monitoring points (where, what to monitor, which sensor) new

The page no longer proposes anonymous "sensors S1, S2, ...". Each proposal is a monitoring point: where (the manhole), what to monitor there (the reading and what trips an alert) and which sensor (the recommended device, alternatives and mounting). Two kinds are placed:

–level sensors (green pins L1, L2, ...): early warning of overcapacity, where the Before surcharge placement puts them
–float switches (yellow pins F1, F2, ...): early warning of a spill, in every manhole the model says can spill
–float locations if the near-spill manholes are added (water within 300 mm of the lid by 100% build-out)
–manholes where the water comes within 500 mm of the lid, so a submersible pressure sensor is recommended over radar

25 October 2026

Simulation 4: warn before surcharge new

A new page, Simulation 4, that re-scores Simulation 3's runs on the question the network operator asks: is a plausible overcapacity detected, and located, before any manhole surcharges? Growth takes years, so "before" is counted in build-out steps (10% to 300% of each area's share of the 2021–41 projection), not minutes. No model was re-run: Simulation 4 reads Simulation 3's published runs and map, and Simulation 3's page is unchanged.

–area build-outs (area × case) overload a manhole by 300%
–warned before surcharge by Sim 4's 5 sensors (rise 10 mm)
–by Sim 3's exact 5 sensors, same rule
–of the runs Sim 3's score credits that set with had already surcharged

Figures are read from simulation4/data/before.js when this page loads, so they cannot drift from the published scoring.

What changed, and why

Limits, carried over and new. Only seven build-out steps, so an overload that jumps from no response anywhere to surcharge in one step cannot be warned of by any sensor (finer steps need new solves). The pipe that causes a surcharge is not recorded, so locating uses the first-surcharge manholes. Greenfield growth is not re-scored. Everything Simulation 3 assumes still holds: uncalibrated, steady state, ideal pumps, thresholds not yet set from real level records. The method is written up in docs/sim3_before_surcharge.md.

Planned next (not built): level sensors with two more roles, both computable from the same runs: tracking the shrinking headroom at the pipes that surcharge first, and guarding the manholes where a spill can occur. See docs/sim4_monitoring_points.md.
