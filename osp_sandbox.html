Observability model

Detection threshold0.05 m
The rise a sensor must see to tell a blockage from ordinary diurnal variation. Not calibratable from public data, so it is a declared parameter.
Lowers the ceiling where a property connection sits below the manhole lid.
Coefficient c0.70
Kept for comparison only. It multiplies pipe diameter, not manhole depth, which is the substitution this rebuild exists to remove.
Allowed invert drop1.00 m
Starved-flow shareoff
Credits a sensor downstream of a blockage, which sees flow stop rather than rise. Zero disables it. The share is how much of the flow past the sensor must halt before it is distinguishable.
