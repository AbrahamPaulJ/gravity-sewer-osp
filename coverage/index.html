Simulation 3's greenfield runs: a new estate of 10, 30, 80, 150 or 700 homes joins at one of the 328 manholes, everything else unchanged, under eight weather cases, solved in EPA SWMM: one run per manhole, size and weather, 13,120 in all. No run adds homes at two manholes, so this page places one estate at a time; several developments at once would need new SWMM runs (Simulation 2.5's scenario builder does that). A manhole "notices" the estate when its peak depth rises by at least the chosen amount, or newly passes 150 mm under the alarm rule. The first ten sensors for each rule and objective are the greedy sets Simulation 3 publishes (this page reproduces their order and scores exactly); later sensors continue the same method over all five sizes. Simulation 3's recommended set comes from its infill runs and the exact solver, so it can differ from this order. Four weather cases (Dry, Design wet and its two variants) are still the fixed 480-minute runs, not re-run until levels settle; they are marked in the weather list. The hydraulic model is uncalibrated, so these are model estimates, not field results.
